NOVA Fashion / Concept project

A collection launch with a live pulse.

NOVA is a fictional premium fashion label preparing a global collection reveal. Vesper's concept turns the launch into an immersive editorial journey, then connects campaign attention to a focused live dashboard.

Challenge
Make a digital collection launch feel like an event.
Solution
Immersive launch platform, spatial product stories and live campaign intelligence.
Demo result
+37% engagement, 2.4x deeper exploration and a 41 ms interface response.
